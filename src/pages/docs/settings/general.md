## Window {#window}

- **Language**: English, Arabic or Kurdish (Sorani), or the same as Windows.
- **Theme**: by default, the same as Windows.
- **Start FloServer when I sign in to Windows**
- **Code editor**: what **Open in VS Code** and the code button open. **Automatic** picks VS Code.

## Updates {#updates}

**Check now** looks for a new version. FloServer also checks once a day and asks before updating. See [Updates](/docs/updates/).

## About and licences {#about}

The version you have, a link to floserver.org, and **Licences**: the notices of the open-source parts.

## Remove FloServer {#remove}

**Remove FloServer…** uninstalls it, and lets you choose what else is deleted. See [Remove FloServer](/docs/remove/).
