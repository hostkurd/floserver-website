## When an update is ready {#ready}

It's downloaded and checked against the signed list first. Then a bar appears in the projects window. Click **Update now**, or **Later**; **What's new** shows the changes.

<figure><img src="/img/docs/main-update-banner.png" width="1084" height="60" loading="lazy" alt="A blue bar: FloServer 0.2.0 is ready, with What's new, Later and Update now"><figcaption>The update bar. The version and size are examples.</figcaption></figure>

## While it updates {#updating}

Your projects stop for a moment. FloServer installs the new version, opens again by itself and starts your projects again. Your projects and databases are kept.

<figure><img src="/img/docs/main-updating-dialog.png" width="478" height="221" loading="lazy" style="max-width:520px" alt="The Updating FloServer window with its four steps"><figcaption>Updating.</figcaption></figure>

## Check now {#check}

To check at once: [Settings → General](/docs/settings/#general) → **Check now**.
